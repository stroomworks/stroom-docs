Guides are task-shaped.
Each one takes a job you might actually need to do and walks through it, ending with how to tell whether it worked and what to do if it did not.

They are ordered by the life of the data: getting it in, turning it into something structured, storing it, and asking questions of it.
That is also roughly the order you learn them in, so reading forwards works.

If you want to understand how a part of Stroom thinks rather than how to operate it, the [Concepts]({{< relref "../concepts/_index.md" >}}) section is the better starting point.
If something is broken, go to [Operate]({{< relref "../operate/_index.md" >}}).
