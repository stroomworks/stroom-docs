Stroom collects, normalises, searches and alerts on machine data.
These pages explain how it does that.

New to Stroom?
**[Get started]({{< relref "get-started/_index.md" >}})** takes you from installing it to a working dashboard.
Then read **[Concepts]({{< relref "concepts/_index.md" >}})**: feeds, pipelines, indexes and state stores are the vocabulary everything else is written in.

**[Guides]({{< relref "guides/_index.md" >}})** are task-shaped: getting data in, translating it, searching it, and turning a search into a rule that keeps running.
**[Reference]({{< relref "reference/_index.md" >}})** is the lists to look things up in, most of them generated from the Stroom source.
**[Operate]({{< relref "operate/_index.md" >}})** is for whoever runs the system, and covers users and permissions, nodes and jobs, and what to do when something is wrong.
