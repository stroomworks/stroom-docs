Reference pages answer "what are the options?" rather than "how do I?".
They are lists rather than explanations, so they are built for looking things up in and for scanning quickly.

You will find every [pipeline element]({{< relref "pipeline-elements.md" >}}) and its properties, every [expression function]({{< relref "expression-functions.md" >}}), every [document type]({{< relref "documents.md" >}}), every [scheduled job]({{< relref "jobs.md" >}}), every [datafeed response code]({{< relref "response-codes.md" >}}) and every [configuration setting]({{< relref "configuration-properties.md" >}}), along with the [query language]({{< relref "stroom-query-language.md" >}}), the [XSLT functions]({{< relref "xslt-functions/_index.md" >}}) and a [glossary]({{< relref "glossary.md" >}}).

If you want to understand how something works rather than what its options are, the [Concepts]({{< relref "../concepts/_index.md" >}}) section explains the model, and [Guides]({{< relref "../guides/_index.md" >}}) walk through the jobs you might want to do.
